Key findings

  1. Proposed shortlist for November 12: 511 S Eastern Blvd, 0 Driveway (off Cliffdale Rd), 5017 Yadkin Rd, 1540 Buster Vee Ln and E Jenkins St. All five are at least 2.5 acres, more than 1,000 ft from a public school, and inside or touching Fayetteville city limits.
  2. 511 S Eastern Blvd is the cleanest in-city site. It is a 3.93-acre former hotel site zoned HI, out of the flood zone, about 1,460 ft from the nearest school and 0.3 mile from transit. Our working figure is about $100,000 per acre; the seller has not confirmed a price.
  3. Flood exposure is the main constraint on the in-city listings. Yadkin Rd (35%), Buster Vee Ln (16%), Driveway (12%) and Duke St (41%) are all partly in the FEMA flood zone and need buildable-area test fits. Mann St fails the 1,000 ft school buffer, and Bonnie St is under 2.5 acres.

    September 9, 2026 environmental findings (F&R)

    • 135 S King St: arsenic in soil above residential and industrial screening levels; benzene, naphthalene and other petroleum VOCs above groundwater standards; chromium or lead in all four groundwater samples; naphthalene above soil-gas screening levels in all seven vapor samples.
    • 110 S Racepath St: TPH-DRO above the NCDEQ action limit; arsenic, cadmium and lead in soil near the oil totes; several metals above groundwater standards; a vapor intrusion risk for residential use.
    • F&R notes that NCDEQ reporting is triggered and that any redevelopment would need a Materials Management Plan.

    An overnight shelter is a residential-type use, so the vapor intrusion finding is the deciding issue. The County expects to terminate before the December 8 deadline and continue with the sites on this page.

    How sites were screened

    Requirements & method

    The County's December 2025 brokerage RFP called for a site inside Fayetteville city limits of at least 3.5 acres, enough for the building, parking and a stormwater retention pond, that is vacant or has removable structures. On October 19, 2026 the Board widened the search to up to five alternative sites within the county, so this survey treats city limits as a preference instead of a disqualifier. For this round the size floor is 2.5 acres and the school buffer is 1,000 linear feet. The spring 2026 feasibility matrix added transit and pedestrian access, utilities, flood risk, environmental history, zoning path, nearby services, and neighborhood compatibility.

    Screening tests on this page

    SizePass at 2.5 acres or more, using both the listed acreage and the County GIS parcel. Marked verify when the two sources disagree across the 2.5-acre line.
    JurisdictionPass if the parcel center is inside Fayetteville city limits (County city-limits layer). Sites elsewhere in the county are marked verify, not fail.
    TransitPass at 0.5 mile or less to a FAST stop, verify at 0.5–1.0 mile, fail beyond 1.0 mile.
    SchoolsPass at 1,000 linear feet or more from a Cumberland County public school parcel, measured parcel edge to parcel edge. Fail under 1,000 ft.
    FloodFEMA flood zone status: Out (Zone X) passes. Partly in shows the share of the parcel in the 1% annual-chance flood zone and floodway, and is marked verify until a buildable-area test fit is done.

    Recommended next steps

    1. Get the seller's price on 511 S Eastern Blvd and confirm the timing to remove the print-shop tenant. Order a Phase I.
    2. Contact the listing agents for Driveway, Yadkin Rd, Buster Vee Ln and E Jenkins St to confirm parcel IDs, total acreage, access, utilities and seller terms. Confirm the E Jenkins auction timing.
    3. Order quick floodway and buildable-area test fits for Driveway, Yadkin Rd and Buster Vee Ln.
    4. Ask City Planning whether the shelter use is allowed under HI, SF-6, CC and AR zoning, and what the special use or rezoning path would be. Ask the County whether E Jenkins St should be annexed or developed under County zoning.
    5. Bring the five-site shortlist to the November 12, 2026 agenda session.
    6. Send the termination notice on the King/Racepath contract before 5:00 PM on December 8, 2026.
    7. Check child care centers and private schools within 1,000 ft of any finalist.
