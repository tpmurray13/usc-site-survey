Finding the right ground for the Unhoused Support Center

The Board's October 19 motion asks staff to identify up to five alternative sites within the county for the November 12, 2026 agenda session. This survey screens 16 properties: 13 from the October land review, two owner leads County staff sent over, and 511 S Eastern Blvd. Each was checked against County GIS, FEMA flood maps and FAST transit data.

Key findings

  1. Proposed shortlist for November 12: 511 S Eastern Blvd, 5017 Yadkin Rd, 1540 Buster Vee Ln, E Jenkins St, and 601–610 Mann St as a building-reuse option. All five are inside or touching Fayetteville city limits and within about 0.7 mile of a FAST stop.
  2. 511 S Eastern Blvd is the cleanest in-city land play. It is a 3.93-acre former hotel site zoned HI at about $100,000 per acre (seller to confirm), with transit 0.3 mile away. The open question is that it sits 0.38 mile from Walker-Spivey Elementary.
  3. Most outside-city listings drop out on transit, not jurisdiction. Six of the ten sites outside the city are more than a mile from the nearest FAST stop, and two more can't be located yet. Bonnie St and 222 Duke St are well located but under the 3.5-acre minimum.

    September 9, 2026 environmental findings (F&R)

    • 135 S King St: arsenic in soil above residential and industrial screening levels; benzene, naphthalene and other petroleum VOCs above groundwater standards; chromium or lead in all four groundwater samples; naphthalene above soil-gas screening levels in all seven vapor samples.
    • 110 S Racepath St: TPH-DRO above the NCDEQ action limit; arsenic, cadmium and lead in soil near the oil totes; several metals above groundwater standards; a vapor intrusion risk for residential use.
    • F&R notes that NCDEQ reporting is triggered and that any redevelopment would need a Materials Management Plan.

    An overnight shelter is a residential-type use, so the vapor intrusion finding is the deciding issue. The County expects to terminate before the December 8 deadline and continue with the sites on this page.

    How sites were screened

    Requirements & method

    The County's December 2025 brokerage RFP called for a site inside Fayetteville city limits of at least 3.5 acres, enough for the building, parking and a stormwater retention pond, that is vacant or has removable structures. On October 19, 2026 the Board widened the search to up to five alternative sites within the county, so this survey treats city limits as a preference instead of a disqualifier. The spring 2026 feasibility matrix added transit and pedestrian access, utilities, flood risk, environmental history, zoning path, nearby services, and neighborhood compatibility.

    Screening tests on this page

    SizeAt least 3.5 acres, checked against County GIS parcel acreage where we could match the parcel.
    JurisdictionPass if the parcel center is inside Fayetteville city limits (County city-limits layer). Sites elsewhere in the county are marked verify, not fail.
    TransitPass at 0.5 mile or less to a FAST stop, verify at 0.5–1.0 mile, fail beyond 1.0 mile.
    SchoolsPass at 0.5 mile or more from a Cumberland County public school campus, verify at 0.25–0.5 mile, fail under 0.25 mile.
    FloodFEMA NFHL zone on the parcel. Any partial AE or floodway area means we need a buildable-area test fit.

    Recommended next steps

    1. Get the seller's confirmation of the roughly $100,000-per-acre pricing on 511 S Eastern Blvd, and a price from Cross Creek New Homes on Mann St.
    2. Contact the listing agents for 5017 Yadkin Rd and 1540 Buster Vee Ln to confirm parcel IDs, total acreage, utilities, and seller terms.
    3. Order a quick wetlands, floodway and buildable-area test fit for both in-city sites.
    4. Ask the City Planning Department whether the shelter use would be allowed under CC and AR zoning, and what the special use or rezoning path would be.
    5. Confirm with the County whether the 0.5-mile school buffer is firm (it affects 511 S Eastern and Mann St), and whether E Jenkins St should be annexed or developed under County zoning.
    6. Bring the five-site shortlist to the November 12, 2026 agenda session.
    7. Send the termination notice on the King/Racepath contract before 5:00 PM on December 8, 2026.
    8. Check child care centers and private schools within 0.5 mile of any finalist.
